# Fnp-Sales-Analysis using MS Excel

## Business Problem
Ferns and Petals (FNP) is an online gifting platform offering products for occasions such as birthdays, anniversaries, Raksha Bandhan, Valentine's Day, Diwali, and Holi. Since customer demand in the gifting industry is highly seasonal and occasion-driven, understanding purchasing patterns is essential for optimizing inventory, marketing campaigns, and operational planning.

The objective of this analysis is to examine historical sales data and identify the key business factors influencing revenue generation. The analysis focuses on customer purchasing behavior, product performance, seasonal demand, geographic distribution, and delivery efficiency to provide actionable recommendations that support data-driven business decisions.

---

## Business KPIs

The first step in any sales analysis is establishing overall business performance through key performance indicators.

- Total Revenue measures the overall financial performance of the business during the selected period.
- Total Orders indicates customer demand and transaction volume.
- Average Customer Spend helps evaluate customer purchasing power and pricing effectiveness.
- Average Delivery Time measures operational efficiency, which directly influences customer satisfaction and repeat purchases.

**KPI Summary**

- Total Revenue: ₹35,20,984
- Total Orders: 1,000
- Average Customer Spend: ₹3,520.98
- Average Delivery Time: 5.53 days

These KPIs provide a high-level view of both commercial performance and operational effectiveness before drilling deeper into individual business dimensions.

---

## 1. Occasion-wise Revenue Analysis

### Why was this analysis performed?
Since FNP operates in the gifting industry, customer purchases are strongly influenced by special occasions. Analyzing revenue by occasion helps determine which events contribute most to annual sales and where marketing investments produce the highest returns.

### Findings
Anniversary and Raksha Bandhan generated the highest revenue, each contributing over ₹6 lakh. In contrast, Diwali and Valentine's Day produced comparatively lower revenue despite being major gifting occasions.

### Business Insight
The results suggest that customers associate FNP more strongly with relationship-based celebrations than with broad cultural festivals. This indicates an opportunity to strengthen festive campaigns through exclusive collections, limited-time offers, and improved promotional strategies during underperforming occasions.

### Business Recommendation
Allocate larger marketing budgets toward proven high-performing occasions while redesigning festive campaigns for Diwali and Valentine's Day to improve customer engagement and revenue contribution.

---

## 2. Product Category Analysis

**Why was this analysis performed?**
Product category analysis was conducted to identify the primary revenue contributors and evaluate dependency on individual product segments.

**Business Insight**
Soft Toys generated nearly 28% of total revenue, making it the strongest-performing category. While this demonstrates high customer demand, excessive reliance on a single category increases business risk if customer preferences shift or market competition intensifies.

**Business Recommendation**
Expanding complementary categories such as personalized gifts, premium hampers, and customized products would create a more balanced revenue portfolio while improving cross-selling opportunities.

---
## 3. Monthly Sales Trend

**Why was this analysis performed?**
Monthly revenue analysis was performed to identify seasonal demand patterns and assist inventory planning.

**Business Insight**
Revenue peaked during March and August, reflecting increased demand around major gifting occasions. Sales declined considerably during April–June and September–November, indicating off-season periods.

**Business Recommendation**
Understanding these seasonal fluctuations enables the business to optimize procurement, staffing, inventory levels, and marketing expenditure throughout the year.

---

## 4.Hour Sales Analysis

**Why was this analysis performed?**
Hourly sales distribution was analyzed to understand customer purchasing behavior throughout the day.

**Business Insight**
Revenue peaks between 10 AM–12 PM and again around 8 PM, suggesting customers primarily shop during work breaks and evening leisure hours.

**Business Recommendation**
These insights can improve advertising schedules, promotional notifications, customer support staffing, and delivery planning.

---

## 5. Product Performance

**Why was this analysis performed?**
Product-level sales analysis identifies best-selling products that contribute significantly to revenue and helps prioritize inventory management.

**Business Insight**
Magman Set generated the highest revenue (₹1.21 lakh), followed by Quia Gift and Dolores Gift.

**Business Recommendation**
These products represent high-demand offerings and should receive priority in inventory planning, promotional campaigns, and bundle recommendations. Mid-performing products can be repositioned through pricing experiments and targeted promotions to increase overall product contribution.

---

## 6. Geographic Analysis

**Why was this analysis performed?**
Geographic analysis helps identify regions with stronger customer demand, enabling targeted marketing and efficient logistics planning.

**Business Insight**
Dhanbad, Imphal, and Kevali recorded the highest order volumes, demonstrating stronger market penetration compared to other cities.

**Business Recommendation**
These locations present opportunities for loyalty programs, faster delivery services, and localized advertising. Lower-performing cities may require awareness campaigns or region-specific promotions to stimulate demand.

---

## 7. Operational Analysis

**Why was this analysis performed?**
Delivery performance directly impacts customer satisfaction and repeat purchase behavior, especially in the gifting industry where timely delivery is critical.

**Business Insight and Recommendation**
The average delivery time of 5.53 days indicates moderate operational efficiency. Reducing delivery time through warehouse optimization, improved courier partnerships, or regional fulfillment centers could enhance customer experience and increase repeat purchases.

---

## Summary

The analysis indicates that FNP's revenue is primarily driven by occasion-based purchasing behavior, with Anniversary and Raksha Bandhan contributing the largest share of sales. Soft Toys emerged as the dominant product category, accounting for approximately 28% of total revenue, highlighting both a strong customer preference and a potential revenue concentration risk.

Seasonal demand patterns reveal significant sales spikes during major gifting periods, emphasizing the importance of aligning inventory planning and marketing investments with peak demand. Geographic analysis identifies cities with consistent purchasing activity that can be targeted for customer retention initiatives, while operational analysis suggests opportunities to improve delivery efficiency.

Overall, the findings support strategic decisions related to campaign planning, product portfolio diversification, inventory management, customer retention, and logistics optimization. The dashboard demonstrates how descriptive analytics in Excel can translate raw transactional data into actionable business insights that support informed decision-making.
